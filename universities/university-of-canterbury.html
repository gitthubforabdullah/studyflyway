Meet University of Canterbury

The University of Canterbury was established in 1873 and is based at Ilam in Christchurch. Its qualifications span creative, scientific and professional fields, with undergraduate and postgraduate options. International applicants can compare direct entry with recognised pathways using the university’s current admission guidance. This profile concerns the New Zealand institution.

Life on campus

Ilam provides a campus setting within Christchurch. Students can compare halls, apartments and private accommodation. Library facilities and study spaces support coursework and research. Clubs, sport and recreation are part of student life, while international support and wellbeing services help students find advice and build connections on campus.

Compare accommodation contracts, room facilities, commute and application dates directly with the provider. Availability and support arrangements can change.
