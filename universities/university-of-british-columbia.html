Meet University of British Columbia

UBC has two main teaching campuses: Vancouver and Okanagan in Kelowna. Both offer undergraduate and graduate study, but their degrees and campus experiences differ. Its academic range includes arts, sciences, applied sciences and professional subjects. Choosing a campus is an important part of choosing a UBC programme.

International admissions

UBC publishes general, country-specific and degree-specific requirements. Select your curriculum, campus and degree before comparing requirements. Graduate applications have separate departmental and Graduate Studies instructions.

Requirements vary by programme. Pakistani applicants should compare academic prerequisites, qualification recognition, language evidence, required documents and deadlines for their intended intake. Postgraduate taught and research applications may follow different routes; contact the university if your qualification is not clearly covered.

Life on campus

Vancouver’s Point Grey campus and Kelowna’s Okanagan campus have distinct surroundings. Campus tours show residences, study areas, lecture buildings and recreation facilities. Libraries, student clubs, sporting activities and international student advising are available through campus services. Housing options and application processes differ by campus, so use the accommodation information for your chosen location.

Compare accommodation contracts, room facilities, commute and application dates directly with the provider. Availability and support arrangements can change.
