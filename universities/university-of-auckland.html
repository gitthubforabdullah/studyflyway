Meet University of Auckland

The University of Auckland grew from Auckland University College, which opened in 1883. It offers undergraduate, taught postgraduate and research study across a broad academic range. The City Campus places many students near central Auckland, while programmes can use other locations, including Grafton. Check the campus attached to the qualification you intend to study.

Life on campus

The City Campus is close to central Auckland shops and transport. The General Library and Kate Edger Information Commons provide study and computing spaces. University accommodation includes different arrangements for new and returning students. Clubs, recreation and sport complement academic study, and international student support offers advice about settling in and studying in New Zealand.

Compare accommodation contracts, room facilities, commute and application dates directly with the provider. Availability and support arrangements can change.
