Meet University of Sydney

Founded as a public institution in 1850, the University of Sydney offers study across the humanities, sciences and professional disciplines. Its Camperdown and Darlington campus brings historic sandstone buildings together with teaching and research facilities. Course location matters: some programmes use specialist campuses or clinical settings elsewhere.

Life on campus

The main campus is part of inner Sydney, with city services and transport nearby. Students can compare university residences, residential colleges and private rentals. Libraries provide study facilities, while student clubs cover cultural, academic and recreational interests. Sydney Uni Sport offers sporting clubs and fitness activities, and university support services help students navigate study and wellbeing.

Compare accommodation contracts, room facilities, commute and application dates directly with the provider. Availability and support arrangements can change.
