Meet McGill University

McGill is a public university founded in 1821. Its downtown campus sits below Mount Royal in Montréal, and its Macdonald campus provides a different setting in Sainte-Anne-de-Bellevue. Students can explore arts, sciences, professional subjects and research degrees. The bilingual city and programme-specific language requirements deserve attention when planning study.

Source: Official university background · Campus information.

Understanding McGill University

McGill's academic range includes law, music, sciences and professional subjects across its downtown and Macdonald campuses. Its Montréal setting and the different character of these campuses are useful considerations alongside the course itself. The academic catalogue should establish where a particular qualification is delivered.

Prepare your application documents · Choose a course

Build your application plan

For a Canadian programme, distinguish the degree, campus and intended major. Ask whether admission is directly to the specialisation or to a broader first-year category. If a work-experience route is offered, investigate selection, scheduling and additional fees separately; do not budget on the assumption that a paid position is guaranteed.

Choose the right study route

  • First undergraduate degree: compare your secondary-school qualifications, required subjects and language evidence with the course requirements. If direct entry is unclear, ask admissions about recognised preparation routes before paying for one.
  • Taught postgraduate study: check the required degree background and relevant modules. Save your transcript, grading scale and course descriptions so you can explain your preparation accurately.
  • Research study: identify a relevant research group, investigate supervisor availability and check whether a proposal is needed. Keep admission, supervision and funding decisions separate.

Keep a course-specific deadline record

Record the application deadline, document deadline, scholarship deadline and offer-response date separately. Include the intake year and the official page where each date appears. If instructions conflict, send admissions the course code and ask which deadline applies to your applicant category.

Life on campus

Downtown Montréal offers an urban setting at the base of Mount Royal; the Macdonald campus has its own student services. McGill residences include dormitory-style and shared-facility options. Libraries and learning spaces support study, while student clubs, events and athletics provide recreation. International Student Services and academic support help students settle into university life.

Compare accommodation contracts, room facilities, commute and application dates directly with the provider. Availability and support arrangements can change.
