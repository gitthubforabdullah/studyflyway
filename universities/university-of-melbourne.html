Meet University of Melbourne

The University of Melbourne combines a broad undergraduate education with specialised graduate and research study. Its original Parkville campus sits close to central Melbourne, while other campuses serve areas such as fine arts, music and agricultural study. Students should compare the undergraduate major and any later professional qualification as separate stages of their study plan.

Source: Official university background.

International admissions

Use the international application guide for your study level. Melbourne assesses qualifications not listed in its recognised-qualifications guidance individually; do not assume a Pakistani school certificate automatically meets direct-entry requirements.

Requirements vary by programme. Pakistani applicants should compare academic prerequisites, qualification recognition, language evidence, required documents and deadlines for their intended intake. Postgraduate taught and research applications may follow different routes; contact the university if your qualification is not clearly covered.

Life on campus

Parkville places students near central Melbourne and its tram network. University accommodation and residential colleges offer different living arrangements. Libraries and study spaces support independent work; student clubs, music and cultural activities sit alongside sport and fitness programmes. Stop 1, Academic Skills and international student support provide starting points for settling in and getting study advice.

Compare accommodation contracts, room facilities, commute and application dates directly with the provider. Availability and support arrangements can change.
