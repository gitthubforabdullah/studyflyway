Meet Monash University

Monash was established in 1958, with its first students beginning at Clayton in 1961. It now teaches across multiple Australian campuses and an international network. The Australian course catalogue spans scientific, creative and professional fields. Applicants should check both the degree and its teaching campus rather than treating every Monash location as interchangeable.

Source: Official university background · Campus information.

Understanding Monash University

Monash has distinct Australian study locations at Clayton, Caulfield, Peninsula and Parkville, as well as an international network. Its location guide associates examples such as business study with Caulfield and nursing with Peninsula. The campus is therefore a core part of a course comparison, not just an address.

Prepare your application documents · Choose a course

Build your application plan

For an Australian course, compare the exact campus, intake and attendance mode on each offer. If a degree includes professional training, check its accreditation and practical requirements for the country where you hope to work. Keep undergraduate study and any later graduate qualification as separate stages in your timeline and budget.

Choose the right study route

  • First undergraduate degree: compare your secondary-school qualifications, required subjects and language evidence with the course requirements. If direct entry is unclear, ask admissions about recognised preparation routes before paying for one.
  • Taught postgraduate study: check the required degree background and relevant modules. Save your transcript, grading scale and course descriptions so you can explain your preparation accurately.
  • Research study: identify a relevant research group, investigate supervisor availability and check whether a proposal is needed. Keep admission, supervision and funding decisions separate.

Keep a course-specific deadline record

Record the application deadline, document deadline, scholarship deadline and offer-response date separately. Include the intake year and the official page where each date appears. If instructions conflict, send admissions the course code and ask which deadline applies to your applicant category.

Life on campus

Clayton is a suburban campus south-east of central Melbourne. Monash Residential Services provides accommodation information, including campus housing and advice about off-campus options. Libraries offer spaces for study, and campus communities organise student activities. Sports facilities and clubs support recreation; student advisers and international support services can help with the transition to university.

Compare accommodation contracts, room facilities, commute and application dates directly with the provider. Availability and support arrangements can change.
