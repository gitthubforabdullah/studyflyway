Meet Monash University

Monash was established in 1958, with its first students beginning at Clayton in 1961. It now teaches across multiple Australian campuses and an international network. The Australian course catalogue spans scientific, creative and professional fields. Applicants should check both the degree and its teaching campus rather than treating every Monash location as interchangeable.

Life on campus

Clayton is a suburban campus south-east of central Melbourne. Monash Residential Services provides accommodation information, including campus housing and advice about off-campus options. Libraries offer spaces for study, and campus communities organise student activities. Sports facilities and clubs support recreation; student advisers and international support services can help with the transition to university.

Compare accommodation contracts, room facilities, commute and application dates directly with the provider. Availability and support arrangements can change.
