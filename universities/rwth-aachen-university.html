Meet RWTH Aachen University

RWTH Aachen is a technical university whose history begins in 1870. Engineering and natural sciences form a substantial part of its academic range, alongside medicine, business and humanities. University facilities are spread across Aachen. The programme catalogue distinguishes degrees, admission conditions and teaching languages, which is especially important for international applicants.

Source: Official university background.

Use the university’s current fee information for your exact programme, study year and fee status. Include compulsory student charges, housing, insurance, course materials and travel in your budget. Scholarship eligibility, coverage and application dates must be checked separately.

Life on campus

Aachen’s university buildings are integrated into the city, with additional facilities outside the centre. Student housing advice covers residences and private rentals. University libraries and learning spaces support study, while student groups and university sport offer activities. The Fit for RWTH programme helps international students prepare for academic and everyday university life.

Compare accommodation contracts, room facilities, commute and application dates directly with the provider. Availability and support arrangements can change.
