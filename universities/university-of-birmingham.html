Meet University of Birmingham

The University of Birmingham received its Royal Charter in 1900. Its Edgbaston campus is the focus of this UK profile, with academic study spanning the arts, sciences and professional disciplines. Applicants should distinguish UK courses from those offered at the university’s Dubai campus when checking admissions, fees and accommodation.

Life on campus

Edgbaston offers a campus setting within Birmingham. University accommodation includes different villages and room arrangements, and students can also investigate private housing. The main library and study spaces support academic work. The Guild of Students, societies and Sport and Fitness activities offer ways to take part in university life; student support services help with wellbeing and practical questions.

Compare accommodation contracts, room facilities, commute and application dates directly with the provider. Availability and support arrangements can change.
