Meet University of Manchester

The current University of Manchester was formed in 2004 by merging the Victoria University of Manchester and UMIST, with predecessor roots extending to 1824. It brings together humanities, science and engineering, and biology, medicine and health. Its Oxford Road setting connects academic buildings with the wider city rather than a single enclosed residential campus.

Life on campus

The Oxford Road university area is connected to central Manchester. Accommodation includes university halls and private rentals, with room types and locations to compare. The University Library and learning spaces support individual and group study. Student societies, sports and cultural activities are part of campus life, and international student services provide practical arrival and study guidance.

Compare accommodation contracts, room facilities, commute and application dates directly with the provider. Availability and support arrangements can change.
