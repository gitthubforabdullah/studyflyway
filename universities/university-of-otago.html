Meet University of Otago

The University of Otago was founded in 1869 and began teaching in 1871. Dunedin is its main campus, with additional teaching and research activity in other New Zealand cities. Its academic divisions cover business, health sciences, humanities and sciences. Applicants should check the location and selection process for their intended qualification, particularly in health disciplines.

Life on campus

Dunedin’s university area supports a substantial student community. Otago offers residential colleges and other university-managed accommodation, alongside private housing choices. Libraries and study spaces support academic work. Student clubs, volunteering and sport provide activities beyond lectures, while international and first-year support services help students adjust to living and studying at Otago.

Compare accommodation contracts, room facilities, commute and application dates directly with the provider. Availability and support arrangements can change.
