Meet University of Edinburgh

The University of Edinburgh was founded in 1583 and teaches across three broad colleges: arts, humanities and social sciences; medicine and veterinary medicine; and science and engineering. Its buildings are spread across several parts of Edinburgh. The teaching location depends on the subject, so check where classes and any clinical or practical work take place.

Life on campus

Edinburgh’s teaching sites sit within a city known for its historic centre and cultural events. Accommodation services publish residence options and application guidance. University libraries and study facilities support academic work. Students can join societies and sports activities, while international applicant support and student services provide advice about arriving and studying in Scotland.

Compare accommodation contracts, room facilities, commute and application dates directly with the provider. Availability and support arrangements can change.
