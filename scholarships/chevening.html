Chevening Scholarships

UK government funding for a one-year master’s degree.

Closed   United Kingdom · Reviewed 19 October 2026
Study levelMaster’s
Funding typeFull funding
DestinationUnited Kingdom

Who should explore this?

For eligible Pakistani applicants to a one-year taught master’s. Chevening requires at least 2,800 hours of work experience gained after completing an undergraduate degree, over a minimum of two years. Applicants must also meet its academic, leadership and return-home conditions.

What does it cover?

Funding is subject to the award terms, including any course-specific tuition limits and allowances.
