Commonwealth Shared Scholarships

Scholarships for selected UK master’s courses linked to development.

Closed   United Kingdom · Reviewed 19 October 2026
SCHOLARSHIP GUIDEUnited KingdomEligibility · Funding · Application steps
Study levelMaster’s
Funding typeFull funding
DestinationUnited Kingdom

Who should explore this?

For eligible applicants, including Pakistani nationals, to an approved master’s course at a participating UK university. Financial need, academic criteria, previous study/work in high-income countries and return-home conditions apply.

What does it cover?

CSC and participating universities jointly fund approved tuition, fares and maintenance under the award rules. Check the current handbook for allowances and exclusions.

Application dates

The official page says the 2026–27 round is closed. It does not establish an open 2027–28 round. Check the next announcement and the participating university’s separate admission timetable.

Checked 2026-10-19. Dates describe the named intake; confirm any later changes with the provider.

Your next steps

  1. Wait for the next confirmed round and approved course list.
  2. Apply for admission to an eligible course as well as the scholarship.
  3. Follow the university’s selection process and CSC’s submission instructions.
