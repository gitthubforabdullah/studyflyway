University of Otago Doctoral Scholarship

Doctoral funding open to domestic and international applicants.

Check dates   New Zealand · Reviewed 19 October 2026
SCHOLARSHIP GUIDENew ZealandEligibility · Funding · Application steps
Study levelPhD
Funding typeResearch funding
DestinationNew Zealand

Who should explore this?

For eligible domestic and international doctoral applicants, normally seeking their first doctorate. Selection considers academic merit and research potential, and quarterly allocations include divisional recommendations. Otago is reviewing the scholarship scheme.

What does it cover?

The published award includes a stipend and domestic-rate tuition support for up to 36 months. Student services fees and insurance are excluded; professional-doctorate tuition support is capped at the domestic rate.

Application dates

Applications can be submitted throughout the year, but assessment is in March, June, September and November rounds. The eVision application and departmental assessment must be complete by the first day of the round month. Confirm the next round and current funding settings with Otago before relying on an award.

Checked 2026-10-19. Dates describe the named intake; confirm any later changes with the provider.

Your next steps

  1. Read the current doctoral funding announcement and award regulations.
  2. Arrange supervision and allow time for departmental assessment before the relevant round.
  3. Submit through eVision using the current scholarship process.
