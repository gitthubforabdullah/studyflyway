DAAD EPOS Scholarships

Funding for selected development-related postgraduate courses.

Check dates   Germany · Reviewed 19 October 2026
Study levelMaster’s / PhD
Funding typeResearch funding
DestinationGermany

Who should explore this?

For eligible graduates applying to a listed development-related postgraduate course. DAAD normally requires a relevant degree and at least two years of related professional experience after the first degree. Degree age, residence history and course-specific language requirements also apply.

What does it cover?

Funding can include a stipend, insurance contributions and travel support under DAAD’s terms. Course duration and any additional benefits vary; do not assume every German degree is covered.

Application dates

The official list covers 2027–28, but there is no single EPOS deadline. Check your chosen course’s scholarship deadline separately from its self-funded admission deadline; some courses have biennial intakes.

Checked 2026-10-19. Dates describe the named intake; confirm any later changes with the provider.

Your next steps

  1. Choose an eligible course from DAAD’s current EPOS list.
  2. Check the course’s scholarship deadline, language evidence and required work experience.
  3. Apply directly to the course using its instructions; applications sent to DAAD are not forwarded.
