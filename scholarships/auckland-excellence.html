Scholarship details

Auckland International Student Excellence

Tuition support for eligible new international master’s students.

Upcoming   New Zealand · Reviewed 19 October 2026
Study levelMaster’s
Funding typePartial funding
DestinationNew Zealand

Who should explore this?

Eligible new international master’s students must have a conditional or unconditional programme offer for Semester One or Quarter Two 2027 by 20 October 2026. Selection follows the university’s invitation process and award regulations.

What does it cover?

The current award is worth 25% of one year’s tuition fees. It supports tuition for one year and is not a full living-cost scholarship.

Application dates

Eligible offer-holders are scheduled to receive invitations on 21 October 2026. The supplementary online form is due 4 November 2026; confirm the exact cutoff with Auckland. Although the page labels the award as requiring no application, its current-cycle instructions require invited students to complete that supplementary form.

Checked 2026-10-19. Dates describe the named intake; confirm any later changes with the provider.

Your next steps

  1. Check your master’s offer and intake against the 20 October eligibility date.
  2. Watch for the university’s invitation and read the regulations.
  3. If invited, complete the supplementary form by 4 November 2026.
