About AusPak International

Education consultancy with an Australian study focus and a Pakistan office network supporting several overseas destinations.

This profile summarises the provider’s published information. Confirm that its current services match your course, destination and application stage.

Study destinations

Australia, Canada, UK, New Zealand and Germany, alongside other destinations published on its website.

Services and application support

Study counselling, course selection, application guidance, visa-process support and arrival support through its Australian office. Ask which services apply to your chosen destination.

Fees and choosing a consultant

Request a current written quote and refund terms directly. Ask which services are included and whether university application charges, test fees and visa charges are separate.

Ask which universities the consultant represents and who controls your application account. Admission, scholarships and visa decisions remain with the relevant institution or authority.
