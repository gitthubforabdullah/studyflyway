About Edify Group

Pakistan-based overseas education consultancy offering university selection, application support and English-test preparation.

This profile summarises the provider’s published information. Confirm that its current services match your course, destination and application stage.

Study destinations

Canada, Australia and UK study support is described in its destination and branch information. Its Islamabad branch also publishes guidance for the USA and Europe; confirm the specific country and programme before booking.

Services and application support

Academic profile assessment, university and course selection, applications, visa-document and interview guidance, pre-departure orientation, and English-test preparation including IELTS and PTE.

Office locations

Faisalabad head branch: Edify Building, 3rd Floor, S1/S2, Susan Road, Madina Town. Lahore DHA: 1st Floor, Plaza Y-213, Phase 3, near ChenOne. Islamabad: Office F1, 1st Floor, Interlace Plaza, I-8 Markaz. Further branches include Lahore Johar Town, Multan, Peshawar and Sargodha.

Fees and choosing a consultant

Request a current written quote and refund terms directly. Ask which services are included and whether university application charges, test fees and visa charges are separate.

Ask which universities the consultant represents and who controls your application account. Admission, scholarships and visa decisions remain with the relevant institution or authority.
