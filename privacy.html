Privacy Policy

StudyFlyway respects the privacy of its visitors. This Privacy Policy explains what information may be collected when you visit StudyFlyway, how that information may be used, and the choices available to you.

StudyFlyway is an independent study abroad information website providing information about universities, scholarships, admissions, visas, study destinations, application processes and international education.

01Information We Collect

Information you choose to provide

Our contact form asks for your name, email address, topic and message. If you submit an inquiry through the enabled form on the deployed website, this information is processed through FormSubmit so the site operator can read and respond. Contact submissions are not public. Form delivery depends on the email recipient activating the form service.

Please do not include sensitive identity, financial or application documents in your message. Browsing StudyFlyway does not require an account. Search and filters run in your browser, and our checklist does not save your selections.

Information collected automatically

Analytics and technical services may process browser and device type, general location such as country or region, pages visited, referring websites, visit dates and times, and basic usage or performance information. Hosting and external service providers may also receive your IP address and standard technical request details when your browser connects to them.

02How We Use Information

Information may be used to operate and improve StudyFlyway, understand how visitors use our content, analyze website traffic, improve the user experience, respond to inquiries, detect technical problems and maintain website security. Contact details are used to handle the inquiry or privacy request you send.

03Analytics

StudyFlyway includes Umami Analytics and Cloudflare Web Analytics to help understand website usage and performance. Reports may include anonymous or aggregated usage data, such as page views, referring websites, browser types and general locations.

Umami describes its standard tracking as cookie-free. Cloudflare Web Analytics measures page performance. These services receive technical information as part of providing analytics; this does not mean that all third-party services on this website are free of cookies.

Read the Umami analytics FAQ and Cloudflare data collection documentation for details about their services.

04Cookies

Cookies are small pieces of information stored by your browser. Websites may use them to support essential functions, remember preferences, measure usage or deliver advertising.

StudyFlyway’s own browsing, search and checklist code does not currently set cookies. Necessary cookies may be used by hosting or security services where applicable. Third-party services, including advertising services when used, may set their own cookies or use similar technologies according to their settings and policies.

You can review, block or delete cookies through your browser settings. Blocking some cookies or scripts may affect third-party features.

05Advertising and Google AdSense

StudyFlyway may display advertisements from third-party advertising partners such as Google. Google AdSense code is included on the website; its presence does not mean advertisements are approved or displayed on every page.

When advertising services operate, Google and its partners may use cookies or similar technologies to deliver, measure or personalize ads, including based on visits to this and other websites. Their use depends on provider policies, settings and applicable consent requirements. Loading third-party advertising code may also send technical connection information to the provider.

Learn more about Google’s advertising technologies and manage relevant preferences through Google My Ad Center.

06Third-Party Links

StudyFlyway links to universities, scholarship providers, government websites, immigration and visa authorities, educational organizations and external consultants. We do not control these websites or their privacy practices. Please review their privacy policies before sharing information.

Some resources load directly from external services: Google Fonts supplies fonts, while Unsplash and Wikimedia Commons supply some images. These providers may receive standard connection information when your browser requests those resources. Our hosting provider may keep technical request logs.

07External Educational Information

Our guides may direct you to official university, scholarship, government or visa websites for requirements and applications. Those websites operate independently from StudyFlyway. Information you enter into their forms is handled by the relevant provider under its own policies, rather than through StudyFlyway’s contact form.

08Data Security

We aim to take reasonable measures to protect information handled through the website, including relying on the security features of our hosting and form services. No method of internet transmission or electronic storage can be guaranteed completely secure. Please share only the information needed for your inquiry and avoid sending sensitive documents.

09Data Retention

Personal information, when collected, should be retained only for as long as reasonably necessary for the purpose for which it was collected, such as responding to an inquiry, or as required by applicable law. Technical and analytics records may be subject to the relevant service provider’s retention settings and policies. No single fixed retention period is specified for every type of information.

10Your Privacy Choices

  • Contact StudyFlyway about information you voluntarily provided and request correction or deletion where applicable.
  • Adjust your browser settings to review, block or delete cookies.
  • Use the relevant provider’s privacy or advertising settings to control available third-party preferences.
  • Choose whether to submit a contact message or follow an external link.

For a request about a previous message, identify the email address used and explain what you would like changed. Do not send additional sensitive documents to prove your identity. The site operator reviews requests in light of applicable requirements, available records and any information that must be retained.

11Children’s Privacy

StudyFlyway is an educational information website and is not intentionally designed to collect personal information from children without appropriate authorization where required. If you believe a child has provided personal information that should not have been collected, please contact us so the matter can be reviewed.

12International Visitors

Visitors may access StudyFlyway from different countries. Hosting, analytics, form and other service providers may process information in countries different from your own. Depending on your location, applicable privacy laws may provide you with certain rights regarding personal information. You can contact us with a request or question about information you have provided.

13Changes to This Privacy Policy

StudyFlyway may update this Privacy Policy from time to time to reflect changes to website features, services or information practices. The “Last Updated” date will change when significant updates are made. Please review this page periodically.

14Contact Us

For privacy questions or requests concerning information you provided, use our contact page. Include enough detail to help us locate the relevant message without sharing unnecessary sensitive information.

StudyFlyway is an independent study abroad information website. Learn more about StudyFlyway or read our disclaimer for the scope of our educational information.
