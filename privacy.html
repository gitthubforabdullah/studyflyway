Privacy Policy

How information is handled on this website.

Browsing StudyFlyway

The website does not require an account. Its search and filters run in your browser. This version does not include advertising trackers or analytics scripts, and does not save your checklist selections.

Contact messages

If you submit the contact form on the deployed website, your name, email address, topic, and message are processed through Netlify Forms so the site operator can read and respond. Do not include sensitive identity, financial, or application documents. Contact submissions are not public.

External services and links

Google Fonts and Unsplash may receive standard connection information when your browser loads fonts or destination images. The hosting service may keep technical request logs. External universities, scholarship providers, and consultants have their own privacy practices.

Your requests

Use the contact page to request correction or deletion of a message you submitted, identifying the email address used. Do not send additional sensitive documents to prove your identity. The site operator is responsible for reviewing and handling requests.

Policy updates

This policy describes the initial website as prepared on 2 October 2026. It should be updated if the operator adds analytics, accounts, advertising, or other data collection.
