Editorial Policy

How we research and maintain StudyFlyway information.

Our sources

We prioritise official university, scholarship-provider, and government pages. Consultant listings describe their own published services and link to their websites; they are not independent assessments of quality.

Dates and status

Content was reviewed on 18 October 2026. We label a scholarship “Open” only where an application window has been identified. “Check dates” means you need to confirm the current round with the provider. Timed labels can close after a known deadline, but new rounds require a manual editorial update.

Scope and corrections

We summarise selected opportunities rather than every available course or award. Requirements can differ by nationality, institution, course, and application cycle. Send corrections through our contact page with a link to the original source.

Commercial relationships

The initial directory has no paid or affiliate listings. Any future sponsored placement should be clearly labelled and must not be presented as an independent recommendation.

Images

The homepage campus image is AI-generated and illustrative; it does not depict named students or endorse a particular institution. Destination photography and attribution are listed on the image credits page.
