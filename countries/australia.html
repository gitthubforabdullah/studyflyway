Get to know Australia

Australia stretches from tropical northern regions to temperate southern cities and a vast inland interior. Canberra is the national capital; Sydney and Melbourne are separate state capitals. Distances between cities can be substantial, so your choice of campus shapes everyday life as much as your choice of university.

A brief history of Australia

Australian history begins with Aboriginal and Torres Strait Islander peoples, whose distinct cultures and connections to land and sea long predate European settlement. Understanding this continuing heritage gives important context to place names, museums and university acknowledgements of Country.

  1. Before European settlement: Indigenous communities developed diverse languages, knowledge systems and trading connections across the continent and surrounding islands.
  2. 1788: The First Fleet established a British colony at Sydney Cove. Colonisation brought dispossession, conflict and profound disruption to Indigenous peoples.
  3. 1901: Six British colonies federated to form the Commonwealth of Australia. Federation created a national government while retaining state governments.
  4. The twentieth century: Migration and urban growth helped shape modern Australia's culturally diverse society. Indigenous rights and recognition remain important parts of its national story.

History sources: National Museum of Australia: defining moments · National Museum of Australia: Federation.

Major cities and student destinations

These cities offer different settings for daily life. This is a selection of destinations, not a population ranking or a complete list of places to study.

Sydney · Melbourne · Brisbane · Perth · Adelaide · Canberra · Hobart

Sydney — New South Wales

A harbour city on the east coast, known for the Opera House, its waterfront and a large metropolitan area.

For your study plans: Check the actual teaching campus before choosing housing: a Sydney address can involve a substantial commute.

Explore University of Sydney

Melbourne — Victoria

Victoria's capital combines historic streets, arts venues and sporting institutions with a broad university presence.

For your study plans: Compare city-centre and suburban campuses separately, including how you would travel to early or evening classes.

Explore University of Melbourne

Brisbane — Queensland

Queensland's capital is built around the Brisbane River in Australia's subtropical southeast.

For your study plans: Consider seasonal heat and rain when planning daily travel and choosing accommodation.

Explore University of Queensland

Perth — Western Australia

Perth lies on Australia's west coast beside the Swan River, far from the major eastern cities.

For your study plans: Include interstate travel in your budget if you expect to visit friends or attend events elsewhere in Australia.

Explore University of Western Australia

Adelaide — South Australia

South Australia's capital is known for its planned city centre, surrounding parklands and cultural festivals.

For your study plans: Match accommodation to your course location, particularly if your programme uses a campus outside the city centre.

Explore Adelaide University

Canberra — Australian Capital Territory

The national capital houses Parliament and major national cultural institutions, offering a different setting from the coastal capitals.

For your study plans: Compare public transport routes and campus facilities when deciding between university housing and private rentals.

Explore Australian National University

Hobart — Tasmania

Tasmania's capital sits beside the River Derwent, with a historic waterfront and nearby mountain landscapes.

For your study plans: Look at the course's campus and timetable before planning where to live or how often to travel to mainland Australia.

Explore University of Tasmania

Before choosing your city

For students arriving from Pakistan, plan around Southern Hemisphere seasons and the specific city rather than a single idea of Australian weather. Ask your university about orientation, student associations, prayer facilities and nearby food options. These services should be checked at campus level.
