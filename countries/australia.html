Your starting checklist

  • Academic certificates and transcripts for your previous study.
  • Identity documents and the language evidence requested by the course.
  • References, a statement, portfolio, or research proposal where required.
  • A calendar for admission, scholarship, and document deadlines.

Australia scholarships for Pakistani students 2027

Planning to begin an Australian degree in 2027? Compare government scholarships and university funding separately. Record the eligible degree level, subject, nationality rules, coverage and deadline for each award. Check whether admission and scholarship applications are separate, and budget for expenses the award does not cover.

Australia Awards 2027 intake: DFAT listed 30 April 2026 as the closing date for Pakistan, so that application round has closed. Read the Pakistan intake information and official opening and closing dates before planning a future application.
