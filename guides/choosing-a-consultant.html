External support

Before you choose a consultant

Questions to ask, details to verify, and how to keep control of your application.

THE STUDYFLYWAY GUIDEGood preparation makes a difference.

Practical steps to help you move forward.

Understand the service being offered

An education consultant may help with course selection or application preparation. Ask which destinations and institutions they work with, which services are included, and whether they receive commission from universities. You can also explore direct application routes on official university websites.

Ask these questions

  • What are all charges, payment dates, and refund conditions?
  • Which institutions do you represent, and how can I confirm that relationship?
  • Who prepares and reviews my application?
  • Will I retain access to my email, application account, and submitted documents?
  • If immigration advice is offered, what authorisation is required and how can I verify it with the relevant authority?

Keep control of your application

Read every document before submission. Keep receipts, written terms, and copies of your application. Verify any claimed scholarship or offer with the issuing institution. No external organisation can guarantee an admission decision, scholarship, or visa outcome.

How our directory works

Our listings link to external organisations. They are not ratings, endorsements, or a statement that we have verified service quality. StudyFlyway does not take consultant payments or application documents on this website.
